What this is. Every ServiceWaze report becomes a tracked receipt with a reference number, the responsible entity, an SLA clock and a public state. Residents confirm each other's reports, so the numbers below are community-verified, not call-centre self-reporting.

Method. SLA hours by fault type: leak 72 h, no water / low pressure 24 h, power 6 h, route 12 h, unsafe place 72 h, other 48 h. "Past SLA" = open and elapsed beyond the SLA.

Privacy. Reporters are pseudonymous device identities — no names, phone numbers or addresses are stored or shown. Data shown with its source; nothing is inferred or invented.

Printable, and downloadable as CSV for a councillor, a ward committee or a municipal depot.
