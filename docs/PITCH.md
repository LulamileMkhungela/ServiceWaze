## 1. The 60-second pitch

> Load-shedding ended. The crisis didn't — it moved into water, roads, food prices and tariffs.
>
> In June 2026 parts of Johannesburg went **twelve days without water**. A household food basket costs **R5 530 a month**. Electricity rose **8.76%** in April and a winter peak unit costs **R8.47** — four times the off-peak price. And the information that could have helped arrived *after* the taps ran dry, in English, inside an app that eats data.
>
> ServiceWaze is built for the two hours **before** the outage.
>
> It fuses live weather, Eskom status, municipal notices and neighbour reports into one countdown — *"water off in 3 h 20 min"* — then hands you a task list sized to the time you have left and to your actual household: fill 150 litres, charge the phones, cool the fridge, check on the gogo next door.
>
> Then it goes further than any status app. It turns neighbour capacity into a grid: a borehole, a tank, a generator, a fridge shelf, a seat in a bakkie — offered, claimed, and counted. It puts real tariffs to work so a family can see that moving the geyser off peak saves R340 a month, and that their roof can catch 900 litres from this week's rain. It turns every complaint into a **receipt with a clock on it**, so a street can see whether its municipality kept its promise.
>
> And it makes preparedness a community sport: a Resilience Score, Ubuntu Points, weekly challenges and street leaderboards — in five South African languages, offline, on a R500 phone, with no login and no data selling.
>
> We don't just tell South Africans what's broken. We make sure they're ready before it breaks, and we can prove how much money it saved them.

## 2. Why we win each category

### Best Consumer Solution
* Solves a weekly, painful, mass-market problem — not a nice-to-have.
* **One thumb, one screen:** the Now tab answers "how long do I have and what do I do?" in under three seconds, with no setup and no account.
* Retention is built in: score, streak, challenges, leaderboards, and a receipt that brings you back when the city is late.
* Free, no ads, no tracking, no paywall on the mission-critical features.

### Most Innovative Solution
* **The Prepare Window** — the first countdown *to* a service disruption rather than a notification *of* one. Competitors notify at impact; we act before it.
* **Time-budgeted task planning** — minutes available vs minutes needed, computed per household. The app tells you when the plan doesn't fit.
* **Provenance-first data** — every value carries source + tier + timestamp, with a public source console. Nobody else in the category does this, and it is why municipalities can trust it.
* **Three-tier fetching** — server → device-direct (CORS) → deterministic demo — so the app is always alive and always honest about it.
* **Ubuntu Grid + stokvels** — mutual aid and group savings as first-class product surfaces, with a reputation layer.
* **Money engine on real tariffs** — load shifting, leak detection, rainwater litres from a live forecast, solar payback.

### Best South African Solution
* Built for SA constraints first: 5 languages shipped (11 designed for), offline PWA, data-saver, USSD and WhatsApp channels, read-aloud for low literacy, works on entry-level Android.
* Uses **South African** reality: Joburg's rising-block water tariff and free 6 kl, Eskom Homeflex/Homepower 2026/27, Cape Town and Tshwane tariffs, PMBEJD food-basket data by city, real municipal fault lines and GBV/Childline numbers.
* Names the responsible entity per area — Johannesburg Water, City Power, JRA, Cape Town Water — and tracks whether they met the clock.

### Best Financial Solution
* The **money engine** is the financial core: bill breakdowns on the real tariff, load-shifting advice worth hundreds of rand a month, appliance-level cost transparency, leak detection that can save 20–40 kl, solar payback maths, and a savings ledger.
* **Resilience stokvels** turn the 2025-winning stokvel insight into infrastructure for survival: neighbours co-fund a tank, a solar kit or a bulk staple buy, with visible progress.
* Group-buying power is the path to real household cost reduction, not a points scheme.

### Best Agricultural Solution
* **Rainwater harvesting calculator**: roof area × live forecast = litres catchable this week, converted into days of water for the household.
* **Food-garden advisories** derived from the forecast (frost, heat, heavy rain), and a food-garden action in the resilience profile.
* Food access mapped from real OpenStreetMap data: markets, food banks, clinics, feeding points.

### Accenture Reinvention Award
* It **re-invents the citizen–utility relationship**: reports stop disappearing into call centres and become public, timed receipts with a ward scorecard.
* It **re-invents the sharing economy for scarcity**: not monetising spare rooms for tourists, but spare litres for neighbours.

## 3. The three-minute demo script (use this on stage)

1. **(0:00) Open Now.** "This is Pimville, 3 hours and 20 minutes before a planned interruption. Watch the countdown, not a status light."
2. **(0:30) Tap the first task.** "Fill 150 litres — the app knows there are five people in this house. Done. That's 40 XP and R4.50 of water that isn't bought from a truck at 4 am."
3. **(1:00) Prepare tab.** "Here's the maths it did for me: I have 200 minutes, the plan needs 41. If it didn't fit, it would tell me what to drop."
4. **(1:20) Money engine.** "Same house: R1 274 a month on electricity, blended R3.64 a unit. Now watch load shifting — off-peak R1.31 versus peak R8.47. Move the geyser, save R340. And their roof will catch 900 litres from this week's rain."
5. **(2:00) Grid.** "A neighbour 400 m away has a borehole. One tap to claim it. That's Ubuntu Points. And here's the street stokvel: R3 150 of R4 500 towards a tank that ends water outages for the whole street."
6. **(2:30) Community.** "Every report becomes a receipt: SW-7K3Q, logged with Johannesburg Water, 24-hour SLA, currently 31 hours — overdue. And here's the ward scorecard: 6 open, 1 fixed, 4 overdue, 100% compliance when they do respond. That's the product a municipality buys."
7. **(3:00) Close.** "Five languages, offline, no login, no tracking. This is what preparation looks like when you build it for the people who can't afford to be unprepared."

## 6. What is real vs. demo in the sandbox

* **Real code paths, real endpoints.** Weather, air quality, geocoding, radar, Eskom status, EskomSePush, Johannesburg Water, Google News, Mastodon and OpenStreetMap Overpass all hit their production URLs.
* **Tariff tables are real and dated** (2026/27), with the source in the response.
* **Demo data** appears only when an upstream is unreachable — and is **badged `DEMO`** in the UI, with the source console showing `sim`.
* `python demo_seed.py` seeds a realistic neighbourhood (offers, stokvels, receipts, neighbours) for judging; everything it inserts is prefixed `demo-` and labelled.
